# LIFE+ ESCAPE Programme — overview

The LIFE+ ESCAPE programme (LIFE11 BIO/FI/000917, 2012–2017) collected and
banked 175 of Finland's most threatened plant taxa, with 148 placed in long-
term seed-bank storage. The programme was led by the University of Oulu
Botanical Garden in cooperation with botanic gardens at Helsinki, Turku, and
Jyväskylä.

The programme prioritised species classified as Vulnerable (VU), Endangered
(EN) or Critically Endangered (CR) on the 2010 Finnish Red List, with
particular focus on:

- Coastal species of the Bothnian Bay (e.g. *Primula nutans*).
- Boreal mire indicator species (e.g. *Saxifraga hirculus*).
- Calcareous fell species of Lapland (e.g. *Antennaria villifera*).
- Esker pine-heath species (e.g. *Pulsatilla patens*, *Pulsatilla vernalis*).
- Old-growth forest indicators (e.g. *Lobaria pulmonaria*).

Seeds are stored at –18°C in moisture-controlled chambers at the Garden's
seed bank facility on the Linnanmaa campus.
