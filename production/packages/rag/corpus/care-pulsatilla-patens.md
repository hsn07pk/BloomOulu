# Care note — *Pulsatilla patens* (kangasvuokko)

## Status

- Finnish Red List 2019: **Vulnerable (VU)**
- LIFE+ ESCAPE programme: included, seeds banked since 2012.
- Oulu Garden accession: OULU-1998-0421 (Hämeenlinna population).

## Habitat in cultivation

- South-facing esker bed with full sun, no shade.
- Free-draining sandy soil, pH 5.5–6.5.
- No irrigation after establishment except during prolonged drought.
- Companion species: *Antennaria dioica*, *Pulsatilla vernalis*.

## Threats in the wild

- Fire suppression on natural eskers has closed the canopy; *P. patens*
  seedlings cannot establish without bare mineral soil that historically
  appeared after natural fires.
- Habitat fragmentation from forestry and recreational use.

## Conservation actions

- Annual seed harvest in late May.
- Cooperation with Metsähallitus on prescribed burning of source eskers.
- Reintroductions to two Häme sites planned for 2026.
