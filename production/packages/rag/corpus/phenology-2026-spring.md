# Phenology log — Spring 2026

## Week 18 (28 Apr – 4 May)
- *Pulsatilla patens* (kangasvuokko) — first violet flowers opening on the south esker bed.
- *Pulsatilla vernalis* (kevätesikko) — buds visible, opening expected next week.
- *Andromeda polifolia* — pink bells beginning in the mire bed.

## Week 19 (5 May – 11 May)
- *Pulsatilla patens* — peak bloom, 47 flowers counted on the south esker.
- *Cypripedium calceolus* (tikankontti) — shoots emerged, no flowers yet.
- *Menyanthes trifoliata* — first flowers in the pond margin.

## Week 20 (12 May – 18 May)
- *Cypripedium calceolus* — flower buds visible, expected to open by week 21.
- *Trollius europaeus* (kullero) — leaf rosettes emerging.
- *Linnaea borealis* (vanamo) — leaves green; no flowers yet.

## Week 21 (19 May – 25 May)
- *Cypripedium calceolus* — first flowers open in the limestone-forest bed. Please stay on the boardwalk.
- *Saxifraga hirculus* — leaves emerging in the mire bed; flowers expected August.

## Greenhouse (Romeo & Julia) — May 2026
- *Victoria amazonica* — leaves expanded; first night bloom expected mid-August.
- *Nymphaea thermarum* — flowering steadily; offspring being transferred to partner gardens.
- *Wollemia nobilis* — healthy, no flowering this year.
