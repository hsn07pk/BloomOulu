Problem Space

Three gaps stand between
the garden and its full impact.

Each gap is measurable, each is documented in our research dossier, and each blocks a different rubric criterion the judges will score.

01
Engagement

Visitors look,
then forget.

QR codes — if present — link to wikis or PDFs. No story, no audio, no follow-up. Curiosity dissipates before it can become support.

~3 min avg dwell time per plant before app fatigue sets in (museum-sector benchmark)
02
Revenue

One €5 button
for 16 hectares.

No tiering, no recurring layer, no corporate route. A €5 ceiling caps what a visitor can give — and how deeply the garden can fund conservation.

€5 → €2,500 the price ladder we will open across five conservation tiers

The Solution · Three Capabilities

BloomOulu turns the garden
into a living digital twin.

One mobile-first platform, three capabilities, one priority. Adopt-a-Plant leads — it is the revenue engine and the public face of conservation. AskTheGarden deepens understanding. Immersive QR is the on-site magic.

P1 · Revenue & Story Engine

Adopt-a-Plant

Symbolic adoption · 5 tiers · €25 → €2,500

Visitors and supporters give a specific endangered Finnish species a name they will remember — and a funding line that scales with rarity.

  • Certificate with photo, story, IUCN status
  • Quarterly grower's notes & seasonal photos
  • Recurring or annual · gift & corporate options
  • Stripe + MobilePay · receipt automation
P2 · Knowledge Layer

AskTheGarden

RAG chatbot · grounded · multilingual

A conservation assistant grounded in the garden's own care notes, accession records, and curator knowledge — never hallucinated.

  • Citation chips on every answer
  • FI · EN · SV launch · audio replies
  • “Plants in bloom this week” awareness
  • Staff knowledge becomes a public asset
P3 · On-Site Magic

Immersive QR

Story-first plant pages · audio · AR-ready

Every plant has a page with audio narration, Finnish & scientific names, and a one-tap path to adopt — the magic moment where curiosity becomes commitment.

  • 3-minute audio stories per featured plant
  • Phenology-aware “What's in bloom”
  • Offline-tolerant in greenhouse dead-spots
  • Visit-loop badges drive return rate
Why this stack

Each capability is built first; together they form the living digital twin — a stack the garden owns, can extend, and can license to peer institutions across the Nordics.

One loop. Five steps.
Curiosity becomes commitment.
